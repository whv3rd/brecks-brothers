About the Service

Brecks Brothers Landscaping is a family-run lawn care operation serving New Castle County, Delaware and surrounding areas. Our commitment is simple: use only battery-powered electric tools — no gas fumes, no excessive noise, no carbon footprint — and only walk-behind mowers that deliver the precision, striping, and professional finish that riding mowers simply cannot match.

Our Differentiators

  • 100% battery-electric equipment
  • Walk-behind mowers only (no riding)
  • Bagged or mulched at client preference
  • Quieter than traditional lawn care
  • Zero emissions — better for neighbors & environment
  • Precision edge work and clean finish
  • Family owned — personally accountable
  • Serving NCC Delaware & surrounding areas

The Eco Difference

Gas-powered lawn equipment is one of the most disproportionate sources of small-engine pollution in residential areas. By operating entirely on battery-powered tools, Brecks Brothers Landscaping delivers a professional result while meaningfully reducing noise pollution and carbon emissions neighborhood by neighborhood.

About the Bakery

Brecks Brothers Bakery is an artisan home bakery delivering fresh-crafted breads, bagels, pizza doughs, and more directly to residents in the Greenville, Delaware area. Everything is made fresh, in small batches, with care — no preservatives, no mass production, and no compromise on quality. We believe everyone deserves access to the kind of bread that makes a difference.

What We Bake

  • New York-style bagels
  • Sourdough & artisan loaves
  • Pizza doughs & focaccia
  • Soft dinner rolls & sandwich bread
  • Seasonal & specialty items
  • Completely configurable orders

How It Works

Subscribe to a weekly delivery plan and configure your order to exactly what your household needs. Every order is baked fresh and delivered locally. Pause, modify, or cancel at any time — we work around your life, not the other way around. Serving the Greenville, DE area with expansion plans tied to demand.

"Local artisan quality, made fresh and delivered locally. Because great bread shouldn't require a trip to a specialty bakery."

The Family

About Brecks Brothers

Brecks Brothers was born from a simple belief: that the best businesses operate like great families. Accountable to each other. Honest about what's working and what isn't. Willing to do the hard things because the people involved actually matter.

"Built like family. Run like a business. That's not just a tagline — it's the operating system."

What started as a vision for a digital consulting firm between brothers-in-law has grown into a portfolio of brands each built on that same foundation. The business partnership may have evolved, but the philosophy never changed.

Based in Wilmington, Delaware, Brecks Brothers serves clients, customers, and communities across the Mid-Atlantic and beyond. We are always looking for the next venture worth building, the next partner worth calling, and the next customer worth delighting.
